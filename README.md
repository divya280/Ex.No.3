# 1. Straightforward Prompting

## Prompt

> Explain how Artificial Intelligence helps doctors diagnose diseases in about 100 words.

## Generated Response

Artificial Intelligence helps doctors diagnose diseases by analyzing medical images, patient records, and laboratory reports with high accuracy. AI algorithms can detect patterns that may not be easily visible to humans, enabling early detection of diseases such as cancer, diabetes, and heart conditions. It also assists in predicting health risks, recommending treatments, and reducing diagnostic errors. By automating repetitive tasks, AI allows healthcare professionals to spend more time caring for patients and making informed clinical decisions.

### Evaluation

- Prompt is simple and direct.
- Response is focused and relevant.
- Suitable for factual information retrieval.

---

# 2. Tabular Format Prompting

## Prompt

> Compare Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Traditional Programming in a table including their definition, data requirement, advantages, and applications.

## Generated Response

| Feature | Machine Learning | Deep Learning | Traditional Programming |
|---------|------------------|---------------|-------------------------|
| Definition | Learns patterns from data | Uses neural networks with multiple layers | Uses predefined rules and logic |
| Data Requirement | Moderate | Very High | Low |
| Advantages | Good accuracy | Excellent for complex tasks | Easy to understand and debug |
| Applications | Spam filtering, Recommendation systems | Image recognition, Speech recognition | Banking software, Calculators |

### Evaluation

- Information is well organized.
- Easy to compare multiple concepts.
- Improves readability and understanding.

---

# 3. Preceding Question Prompting

## Prompt

> Why is Artificial Intelligence becoming important in healthcare? Explain how AI improves disease diagnosis and patient care.

## Generated Response

Artificial Intelligence is becoming important in healthcare because it improves diagnostic accuracy, speeds up medical analysis, and supports better decision-making. AI analyzes large volumes of patient data, detects diseases at an early stage, and recommends personalized treatment plans. It also assists doctors by reducing manual work, monitoring patients remotely, and improving hospital efficiency. These capabilities lead to better patient outcomes and more effective healthcare services.

### Evaluation

- The first question provides context.
- The second question receives a more detailed and focused response.
- Produces logically connected answers.

# Comparison of Prompting Techniques

| Prompt Type | Purpose | Output Style | Best Use Case |
|--------------|---------|--------------|---------------|
| Straightforward Prompting | Ask direct questions | Descriptive answer | Definitions and explanations |
| Tabular Format Prompting | Organize information | Table | Comparison and summarization |
| Preceding Question Prompting | Provide context before asking | Detailed explanation | Analytical questions |
| Missing Word Prompting | Test knowledge | Short answer | Fill-in-the-blank exercises |

---

# Advantages

- Improves communication with AI models.
- Generates accurate and structured responses.
- Saves time while obtaining information.
- Helps in education, research, business, and healthcare applications.

# Conclusion

Different Prompt Engineering techniques produce different styles of responses. Straightforward prompting is useful for direct information, tabular prompting presents structured comparisons, preceding question prompting provides contextual and detailed explanations, and missing word prompting is effective for knowledge assessment. Selecting the appropriate prompting technique improves the quality and usefulness of AI-generated responses.
